# TV channel logos for the Plezy fork

Logos for the channel chips and lists of the fork (GPL-3.0). Each `<slug>.png` is transparent, trimmed, at most 512 px wide and at most 40 KB, and reads on a dark background. Where the official artwork is dark, `<slug>.png` is a light version made only by recolouring the official artwork, and the official colours are kept as `<slug>-original.png`. Nothing was redrawn.

All logos are trademarks of their broadcasters. Collection date: 2026-10-22. The first 14 rows are the current channels, the rest are the planned ones in the requested order.

Recolour modes: "single-colour white" turns every opaque pixel white and keeps the alpha channel. "dark parts recoloured to white, colours kept" turns only near-black pixels white and leaves coloured parts untouched, so red boxes or yellow plus signs stay in brand colour.

## Licence picture

- Wikimedia Commons files tagged public domain are tagged so because the logo is a simple text or shape logo below the threshold of originality in its country. That is a copyright statement, not a trademark licence. Most of them also carry a Commons trademark tag.
- Commons files under CC BY-SA 4.0 (attribution and share-alike apply): RTS Play, M6+, ABC iview, ARD Mediathek, 3Cat, SVT Play, TVP VOD.
- tv-logo/tv-logos has no licence file. Its README says the owner does not hold the logos, that they are trademarks, and that the collection relies on CC BY-SA and CC BY-NC-SA terms. Treat these files as unlicensed trademark artwork.
- iptv-org/database is under the Unlicense, but it only stores pointers to images on third-party hosts; the image itself has no licence.
- Files taken from a broadcaster's own site carry no licence statement. They were used only as the public site logo.
- Before shipping a release, check each broadcaster's brand-use terms, in particular for the rows from community packs and broadcaster sites.
